&#x20;5. Shipping Mode Performance Analysis



\*\*Business Question:\*\* Which shipping mode receives the most orders and generates the highest sales and profit?



Key Findings:



\* Standard Class recorded the highest number of orders (2,994), sales ($1,358,215.74), and profit ($164,088.79).

\* Second Class ranked second, generating $459,193.57 in sales and $57,446.64 in profit.

\* First Class generated $351,428.42 in sales and $48,969.84 in profit.

\* Same Day had the lowest order count (264) and sales ($128,363.13).

\* All four shipping modes generated positive overall profit.



Business Recommendations:



\* Maintain the Standard Class shipping option because it contributes the largest share of business volume.

\* Investigate why Same Day has fewer orders and evaluate whether its pricing or delivery costs affect customer adoption.

\* Compare profit per order across shipping modes before making decisions about shipping strategy.
